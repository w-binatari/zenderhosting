The fastest supplier payments start with a complete first message. Before you contact your FX or treasury desk, have these ready.

## The checklist

1. **Invoice or proforma invoice** from the supplier.
2. **Amount and currency** exactly as billed (for example, USD 25,000 or CNY 180,000).
3. **Deadline:** the date the supplier needs to receive the funds.
4. **Supplier details:** legal name and country, as they should appear on the payment.
5. **Payment purpose:** goods, services, deposit, or balance.
6. **Your contact details:** a work email and a phone or WhatsApp number.

## Nice to have

- Whether the supplier accepts a second currency (for example, dollars instead of yuan).
- Whether this is a one-off or a recurring payment.
- Any earlier payments to the same supplier, for reference.

## Why it matters

A complete message lets the desk reply with an indicative level and the right next step straight away, instead of a round of follow-up questions while your supplier waits.
